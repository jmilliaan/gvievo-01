Phantom obstacle in the local costmap

Why run 4 stood still for 3 min 11 s at s12 with nothing in front of it, and why clearing the costmap freed it. The local costmap is a 5 m × 5 m grid around the vehicle, built only from the laser scan. A cell becomes an obstacle when a beam hits it, and is cleared only when a beam passes through it.

Vehicle (1.6 × 0.7 m) Scanner (0.96 m ahead of axle) Scanner view ±95° Blind zone Obstacle cell in the costmap Projected outline that collides Scanner safety fields Person or object
Why the retries never ended. The executor decides the path is clear from the scanner's safety fields. Nav2's controller decides it is blocked from the costmap. Marks in the blind zone satisfy one and not the other, so the vehicle retried 72 times without moving.
What the code does now (HEAD, not yet tested on the vehicle). The executor clears the local costmap itself after a blocked-path hold has lasted 10 s, and whenever Continue releases a wait. Anything really in front is re-marked within 0.2 s.
The trade-off. Clearing also forgets a real object beside or behind the vehicle, and nothing will re-mark it. The hardware safety fields look forward only too. A tight arc that swings the flank or the rear into something unseen is caught by neither.
Settings behind this. Costmap: obstacle and inflation layers only, no saved-map layer, from /scan_gated. Marks up to 5 m, clears up to 6 m, 5 updates per second. Controller: Regulated Pure Pursuit with collision detection, looking about 1.07 s ahead, and a failure tolerance of 0.3 s.
